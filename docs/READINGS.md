# Two blind readings, in full

The line-up behind "One model, one brief, one idea" in [`EVIDENCE.md`](EVIDENCE.md): three films, read on 13 November 2026 by two
readers from two families of model. Each reader was given the brief in [`EYES.md`](../skills/video-decompiler/EYES.md) word for
word, one added line on the size of the tiles, and one folder of sheets made by `vd pack`: every film at two frames a second,
and every frame of its five busiest seconds. Neither was told what the films were, or that two came from one brief. The
letters were dealt differently for each reader, so each key is given first. Their words are unchanged; only their headings are set two levels lower.

## Reader 1: Claude Opus 5.5

In this reader's line-up, **A** was test 5, **B** was test 3 and **C** was Plus One.

#### 1. Ranking (motion design craft, best first)

**1. A (Larder, ~32 s) · 2. B (Larder, ~31 s) · 3. C (Plus One, ~38.5 s)**

**Why A is above B.** A and B sell the same product with nearly the same script ("What's for dinner…", the list rewriting itself, "Sam's got it."), so they compare directly.
- **Swap scene.** A's swap is one continuous move. The chosen pizza grows out of its thumbnail while the old plate shrinks away left (A move-3 f664–f673). The new title then writes in "Chicken", then "& spinach pizza" (f673–f690). B's swap flickers the hero photo through four dishes in about 1.5 s: burger, a blurred risotto, roast chicken again, then ramen (B story 11.50 → 13.00). It reads as indecision, not a choice.

- **Crossfades.** B's are muddier. "Roast chicken" shows twice, stacked and ghosted, at B move-2 f488–f490. The phone shrinks into the "15" tile as a semi-transparent double image at B move-4 f1393–f1400.
- **Holds.** B holds the finished grid nearly still from about 23.5 s to 25.6 s (B move-5 f1506–f1537 barely change). A has no comparable dead stretch before its end card.

**Why B is above C.** C is the more ambitious film, and its art-skin carousel is the best idea in the lineup. As motion it is less clean.
- **Fireworks over type.** Bursts run across the "20" and "in." for most of a second (C move-5 f1928–f1945).

- **B: freelance motion designer, low-to-medium confidence.** It is competent and clean, but it uses generic moves (fade in, slow push, hold). What pushes me there most is the long static grid hold near the end, plus crossfades that ghost instead of morphing.
- **C: freelance motion designer, low confidence.** The art direction is beyond a hobbyist: per-artwork colour grading and type treatments. ## Reader 2: GPT-6.1 Sol

1. RANKING

A above B: A handles more complicated spatial choreography with better continuity. In A/move-1, f758–817 (12.63–13.62s), the invitation carousel changes its foreground card while maintaining depth, overlap and a clear focal plane. Its selected pizza grows out of the alternatives while the spaghetti and other choices recede (B/move-3, f659–682, 10.98–11.37s).
