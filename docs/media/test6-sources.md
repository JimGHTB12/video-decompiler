# Test 6's film: sources and licences

This is the film's own record, from its film folder's `media/food/SOURCES.md` (the paths are those of that folder). All eleven licences were read again in Wikimedia Commons' API (`extmetadata.LicenseShortName`) on 16 November 2026 before the film was put here: all eleven are `CC0`.

Every photograph in this film is from Wikimedia Commons, taken only where the file's own record gives `extmetadata.LicenseShortName` = `CC0` (16 November 2026). Each was fetched as Commons' 1280 px rendering of the file and cut in the film folder: the dishes to a 720 px square round the food (shown round, as plates), the market to 1920×1080 (`market.jpg`) and a blurred, slightly darkened copy (`market-soft.jpg`). CC0 asks for no credit, and the photographers credited on Commons are named below anyway.
